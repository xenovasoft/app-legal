Delete your Becoming You account

How to permanently delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email xenovasoft@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Becoming You account”.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, username, email address and password.
  • Your habits, behaviours, tasks, check-ins, identities and badges.
  • Your journal entries, photos, audio and voice recordings.
  • Your group memberships and the messages you posted in groups.
  • Your notification tokens and coach conversation history.

What may be kept

Records we are required to keep by law, such as payment records held by Stripe for purchases made on the website, are kept only as long as the law requires.

When

Deletion in the app is immediate. Requests by email are confirmed and completed within 30 days.
