Common questions

How do I start? Tap Start free, create an account with your email, and confirm the email we send you.

I can't sign in. Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to get a reset link by email.

Who can see my habits and journal? Only you. In a group you choose exactly what to share, and your name is shown only if you turn it on.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile and use Delete account at the bottom. Your account and data are removed.
