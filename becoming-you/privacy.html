Privacy Policy

Becoming You · Effective 27 October 2026

Becoming You is a personal growth app. Much of what you put into it is private reflection, and this policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and the control you have over it. It applies to the Becoming You apps for iOS and Android and the website they load.

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, username, email address and password.
  • Your growth work: the identities you are building, your habits, behaviours, tasks, daily check-ins and completions, reminders you set, and the reflections you write when you finish a cycle.
  • Journal: the journal entries you write.
  • Videos: identity videos you choose to record or upload.
  • Voice: if you use the custom coach voice feature, the audio recording you provide is used to create a voice for your coach.
  • Groups: groups you join, the habits you choose to share with a group, group messages, cheers and join requests.

Privacy inside groups

Your name is shown to other group members only if you turn on “show my name”. If you turn it off, your existing messages stop showing your name too. Only the habits you choose to share are visible to a group. Your journal and private habits are never shared.

Information collected automatically

  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token from Apple or Google so we can send you notifications.
  • Basic technical data such as request logs, needed to run and secure the service.

We do not use advertising identifiers and the app contains no third-party advertising.

How we use information

  • To run your account and track your progress.
  • To power the AI growth coach when you use it.
  • To run groups and deliver group messages.
  • To send notifications and account emails such as verification and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help run the app:

  • OpenAI — generating your AI coach's responses, when you use the coach.
  • ElevenLabs — creating and playing your coach's voice, when you use voice features. This includes the voice recording you provide for a custom voice.
  • Stripe — payments for paid plans. Card details go straight to Stripe; we keep only a customer and subscription reference.
  • Resend — sending account emails.
  • Google — file storage for uploaded videos and media.
  • Apple and Google — delivering push notifications.
  • Our hosting provider — storing the service's data.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. This removes your account and your data, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Children

Becoming You is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
