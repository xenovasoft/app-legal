Delete your FIRE account

How to permanently delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile, tap Request Account Deletion, optionally tell us why, and confirm.

Without the app: email xenovasoft@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my FIRE account”.

What is deleted

Your account and all data associated with it, including:

  • Your account, name, username, email address, password and profile photo.
  • Your stored documents and the share links you created for them.
  • Your trusted contacts.
  • Your forum posts, reports and inbox messages.

What may be kept

Records we are required to keep by law are kept only as long as the law requires.

When

An administrator reviews each request and contacts you within 48 hours. Your account and data are then deleted, and every request is completed within 30 days.
