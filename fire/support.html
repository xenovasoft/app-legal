Help with the FIRE app for iPhone and Android

Contact us at xenovasoft@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I start? Tap Sign Up, create an account with your name and email, and confirm the email we send you.

I can't sign in. Use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen to get a reset link by email.

Does FIRE call emergency services? No. FIRE is a preparedness and information tool. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Does FIRE track my location? Location is optional. If you allow it, it is used only while the app is open, to show resources and alerts near you.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile and tap Request Account Deletion. See Delete your account.
