Delete your Inspector Frank account

How to permanently delete your account and data

To request deletion, email xenovasoft@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Inspector Frank account”.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email, phone number and password.
  • Your saved details and preferences.
  • Your messages and notification tokens.

What may be kept

Completed inspection orders, reports and payment records may be kept where the law or our obligations to the other party in an inspection require it, and only for as long as required.

When

We confirm your request and delete your data within 30 days.
