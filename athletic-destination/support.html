Athletic Destination support

Help with the Athletic Destination app for iPhone and Android

Contact us at xenovasoft@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Use the email and password you registered with. If you have forgotten your password, email us from your account's address and we will help you regain access.

I'm not getting notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Athletic Destination in your phone's Settings, then sign out and sign back in to register the device again.

How do I control who can find me? Open your profile and edit the Recruiting section to choose whether you are open to recruitment.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account, or follow these steps.
