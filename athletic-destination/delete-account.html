Delete your Athletic Destination account

How to permanently delete your account and data

To request deletion, email xenovasoft@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Athletic Destination account”.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email and password.
  • Your profile, photos, bio, sports details and location.
  • Your posts, stories, comments, likes, messages, workouts and recruiting information.
  • Your notification tokens and AI conversation history.

What may be kept

Records we are required to keep by law, such as payment records held by Stripe, are kept only as long as the law requires.

When

We confirm your request and delete your data within 30 days.
