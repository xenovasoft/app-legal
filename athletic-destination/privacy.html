Privacy Policy

Athletic Destination · Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Athletic Destination ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Athletic Destination apps for iOS and Android and the website they load.

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form.
  • Profile: profile and cover photos, bio, the sports you play, position, school, team, goals, skill level, and details specific to your role (athlete, coach, trainer, organization, practitioner and so on).
  • Location: a location name you enter and, if you tap “Use my location”, your device’s coordinates at that moment, saved to your profile so the app can show people and places near you. Other members can see how far away you are, and the coordinates saved to your profile may be available to other members’ apps. You can clear your location from your profile at any time.
  • Content: posts, stories, photos and videos, comments, likes, messages, community posts, workouts, and recruiting or resume information you add.

Information collected automatically

  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token from Apple or Google so we can send you alerts about follows, likes, comments, messages and recruiting interest.
  • Basic technical data such as request logs, needed to run and secure the service.

We do not use advertising identifiers and the app contains no third-party advertising.

How we use information

  • To run your account and show your profile and content to the people you choose.
  • To connect athletes, coaches, trainers and organizations, including recruiting features you opt into.
  • To send notifications and account emails such as password resets.
  • To provide AI features (AD Coach) when you use them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

AI features

AD Coach is optional and asks for your consent first. When you use it, the text you send and the relevant parts of your profile are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate a response.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help run the app:

  • OpenAI — AI responses, only when you use AD Coach.
  • Stripe — payments for paid plans on our website (paid plans are not sold inside the mobile apps). Card details go straight to Stripe; we keep only a customer and subscription reference.
  • Resend — sending account emails.
  • Apple and Google — delivering push notifications.
  • Our hosting provider — storing the service's data.

Content you post publicly, and your profile, can be seen by other members according to the visibility settings you choose.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time — see Delete your account. We remove it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Children

Athletic Destination is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove profile information and content in the app at any time.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Choose whether you are open to recruitment.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or its deletion, by contacting us.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
